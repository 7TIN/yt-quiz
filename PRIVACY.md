# Privacy Policy — Learning Loop for YouTube

**Effective: 18 October 2026**

Learning Loop for YouTube is a browser extension that turns a YouTube video into a study
session: chapter summaries, questions, hints and "did you know" pop-ups, all generated by
Google's Gemini API using an API key that *you* provide.

## What the extension stores on your machine

Everything it keeps lives in `chrome.storage.local` inside your own browser profile:

- **Your Gemini API key**, so the extension can call Gemini for you.
- **Your settings**: model ID, level, quiz frequency and the two density choices.
- **A cache** of generated content — summaries, question sets, pop-ups and answer verdicts —
  keyed by video, language and level, so the same video is never generated (and never billed)
  twice.
- **Learning progress**: which questions of a video you have answered and their verdicts.

## What leaves your browser

- **To Google's Gemini API only** (`generativelanguage.googleapis.com`), and only when you press
  *Start learning*, answer a free-response question, or ask for a fresh set: the relevant part of
  the video's transcript, the chapter titles, and — when you answer — the words you typed, so the
  model can judge them. These calls go straight from your browser to Google with your own API key
  in the request header.
- **Nothing is sent to the developer** of this extension. There is no backend, no account, no
  analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The developer never sees
  your key, your videos, your answers or your progress.
- **Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.** No data is transferred to third parties other than
  Google's Gemini API, which processes the request to return your summary, question or verdict
  under [Google's own privacy terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What the extension reads from YouTube

While you are on a YouTube watch page it reads that page's own public data — the video's ID,
title, chapters and its transcript/captions — to build the learning content for the video you are
watching. It does not read or act on any other site, and it does not browse on your behalf.

## Deleting your data

- **Clear cache** in the extension's settings removes every generated summary, question set,
  pop-up and verdict.
- **Remove key** in the settings deletes your API key from the browser immediately; from then on
  the extension makes no network calls.
- **Uninstalling the extension** removes everything it stored, including the key, the cache and
  your progress, because it all lives in the extension's own local storage.

## Children

The extension is a study tool, not a service with accounts. It collects no personal information
for the developer and is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is committed to this repository with a new effective
date.
